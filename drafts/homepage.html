The Next Chapter of Mobility in India

Technology at Maruti Suzuki is not a feature layer, it is the operating system of the company. We run a full-stack mobility platform that spans hybrid and electric powertrains, connected-car telematics, and data platforms that learn from millions of real-world journeys, using AI, cloud, and in-house tools to turn innovation into safer, cleaner, more efficient drives.
